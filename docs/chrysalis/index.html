What the measured experiment demonstrated

On the Android/Termux handset, network throughput was physically measured. Under the controlled 1-second resource window, Chrysalis selected KEEP, STRUCTURE and DETAIL while voiding RESIDUAL. In that constructed workload, 53.3% of candidate payload bytes were excluded while 97.5% of the experiment's assigned utility was retained.

Commercial hypothesis: under real constrained compute/network workloads, EDEN should spend scarce resources on the information that contributes most to the required outcome.
